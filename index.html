01Introduction

A world told entirely in words.

A MUD — Multi-User Dungeon — is the ancestor of every online RPG you've ever played. Picture a chat window that is also a forest, a tavern, a dragon's hoard. You type look and the world describes itself back to you.

Viking MUD has been running since 1991, originally out of Trondheim, Norway. The world is rooted in Norse mythology and built room-by-room by a small team of volunteer wizards. There are no microtransactions, no battle pass, no AI-generated content. Every description was typed by a human who cared.

You can play in any terminal, in your browser, or with a dedicated MUD client. The barrier to entry is low; the depth is not.
